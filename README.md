# Hit Joe For Brokémon

# What is the game about?
This is a clicker game where you need to hit 'Joe' to get money, with your money you can buy Brokémon cards, Pack upgrades or hit upgrades.
Inside of those packs you can find rare cards sometimes that will give you permanent click boosts and passive income.
Can you collect them all?

### Bug fixes
if your game has some issues, probably its not your fault, before commenting on the issue tab, please check this list;
1. If your save slots keep saying 'loading...' just rename one of your slots (you can also rename it but don't change the name at all), and probably everything will load perfectly!
2. If you don't get passive income from your cards, just reload the game!
3. If your money says NaN you can't buy anything anymore but that can only happen if you have infinity money and get hit back by Joe, then you have to delete the slot and start over... no fix for this, sorry!

If your game has another issue or one of those but the fix doesn't work, just add it in the issues tab, for me to give the most help i can, it is great if you include pictures or even better a video of the crash. Than i'm pretty sure i can always help! Thanks!
